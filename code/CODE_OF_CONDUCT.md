# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0). Summary below; the linked text is the reference.

## Our pledge

Make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on code, not on people.
- Respect the clean-room rule and confidentiality: never post employer, customer or production data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome attention.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

- Applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when representing the project.
- Report incidents privately to the maintainer listed in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
- Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
